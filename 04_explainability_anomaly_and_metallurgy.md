## Important interpretation rule

Mean absolute SHAP tells us the magnitude of model contribution.

It does not by itself tell us:

- physical causality
- that changing the variable will definitely change the defect rate
- that the model relationship is a metallurgical law

Therefore the project uses the language:

```text
model-influential factor
likely contributing factor
engineering review
```

rather than claiming proven causation.

## Observed anomaly associations

| Measure | Anomaly group | Normal group |
|---|---:|---:|
| Defect = Yes | 40.41% | 33.14% |
| Porosity = Yes | 42.48% | 36.41% |
| Scrap = Yes | 23.89% | 22.33% |
| Mean Yield | 78.69 | 81.26 |

The anomaly group shows higher observed Defect/Porosity rates and lower mean Yield.

Scrap shows much less separation.

These are observed associations, not causal conclusions.
